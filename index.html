A two-checkpoint organic-search review

This reference was prepared and self-published by Xingrun Digital. The two links below point to Xingrun Digital's own guides; they are not independent endorsements or guarantees of rankings, traffic, or enquiries.

Checkpoint 1: separate the failure layers

When a site receives little organic traffic, inspect discovery, indexing, relevance, authority, and conversion as separate layers. Confirm final status codes, canonical destinations, robots directives, rendered copy, sitemap membership, and internal-link paths before deciding that the answer is simply more content. The guide to diagnosing a website with little organic traffic expands this sequence.

Checkpoint 2: read the page as a decision surface

When relevant traffic reaches a service page but enquiries remain weak, check whether the page identifies its audience, makes the offer concrete, answers constraints, shows credible evidence, and provides a proportionate next step. Review the mobile contact path and event measurement before blaming the traffic source. Use the service-page conversion checklist to make that review repeatable.

For both checkpoints, record the source date, the observed fact, the proposed explanation, and the next decision separately. This keeps a short review auditable and makes the next comparison more useful.
